ConoCancer is a bilingual tutoring platform designed to educate Spanish and English speakers about breast cancer. 
Our mission is to improve accessibility and reduce misinformation by providing reliable, culturally informed educational content.

Critical information can often get lost in translation, but with ConoCancer, that is no longer a barrier. 
Our platform makes it easy for users to switch between English and Spanish and even supports code-switching, 
the natural practice of alternating between languages within a conversation. By embracing code-switching, 
we allow users to focus on learning and engaging with content rather than struggling with language barriers.

Video-Based Learning:
ConoCancer is centered around interactive, video-based learning. Users can:
Watch educational videos in either English or Spanish
Answer questions in their preferred language, or a combination of both.

AI-Powered Multilingual Interaction:
For short-response questions, we integrate Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) to support:
Multilingual responses
Code-switched speech inputs
This allows users to speak their answers naturally instead of being limited to typing.

Personalized Learning & Progress Tracking:
Users can:
Track their learning progress through videos and quizzes
Follow personalized learning paths based on their performance
Our structured database organizes content by:
Category
Question difficulty level
This enables personalized recommendations and adaptive learning experiences.
